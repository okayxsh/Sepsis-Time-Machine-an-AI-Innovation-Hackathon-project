01 · The problem

Sepsis is a race against the clock and we keep starting late.

Sepsis happens when the body's response to an infection starts damaging its own organs. Caught early, it is treatable. Caught late, it kills.

~11M

deaths a year worldwide from sepsis

1 in 5

of all deaths globally are linked to sepsis

Every hour

of delay in treatment raises the risk of death

“Why?”

Existing rule-based alarms miss many cases, fire constantly, and never explain themselves

Sources: Rudd et al., Lancet 2020 (Global Burden of Disease); Surviving Sepsis Campaign guidelines.
02 · The idea

Predict. Explain. Act.

Three steps that turn hourly ICU data into a decision a clinician can trust.

STEP 1 · PREDICT

Risk every hour

A gradient-boosted model reads 75 features built only from past and current data (vital-sign trends, shock index, lab results) and scores sepsis risk hours before clinical onset.

LightGBM · real ICU data
STEP 2 · EXPLAIN

The top three reasons

Every alert shows the top three reasons in plain English with real values, e.g. “Shock index 1.15 (was 0.80 six hours ago)”. No black box.

SHAP TreeExplainer
STEP 3 · ACT

The golden hour

One click writes an SBAR page to the on-call doctor. The Hour-1 Bundle checklist starts a countdown and suggests fluids or vasopressors based on the patient's values.

LLM + offline fallback

05 · Built to be trusted

Honest by design.

No data leakage

The train/test split is by patient. Every feature uses only past and current hours. The label is never an input.

The AI doesn't invent anything

The LLM only rephrases values we computed. If the API is down, a deterministic template takes over, so it runs fully offline.

We show our misses

The dashboard includes a case the model missed. The threshold slider shows the trade-off between catches and false alarms.

Standards-based actions

The Hour-1 bundle follows the Surviving Sepsis Campaign: lactate, cultures, antibiotics, 30 mL/kg fluids, vasopressors.

Known limitations

Retrospective data from two US hospitals. Not prospectively validated and not yet tested on UAE populations.

Clinician in the loop

Decision support, not diagnosis. The doctor makes the final call, with better information and sooner.
